PROJECT_002 — New
Thikana Cam — GPS Photo Camera
A GPS camera that runs in the browser and installs like an app. Every photo is stamped with the place name, address, coordinates, a mini map, date, time, weather and compass direction. Choose a Full, Compact or Minimal stamp, switch the address between English and हिन्दी, edit the date and time, or stamp an existing gallery photo with a location you pick on the map. Photos stay on the phone — no accounts, no ads, no uploads — and location metadata inside the file can be turned off before sharing.
